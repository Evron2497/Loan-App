Tech-Star Enterprise — Okoa Comrades

Effective date: [Insert effective date]

Tech-Star Enterprise, operating the Okoa Comrades lending service, respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly in accordance with applicable Kenyan data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

To process loan applications and provide our services, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, county, identification details permitted by our verification process, employment information, declared income, expenses, existing debt, loan application records, repayment information, payment references, and relevant account-security and audit records.

We aim to collect only information that is necessary for the purposes explained in this notice. We will not ask you to disclose your M-Pesa PIN, account password, or other confidential authentication credentials to our staff.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your customer account.
  • Assess affordability and evaluate loan applications.
  • Verify identity and help prevent fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Prepare loan offers, agreements, repayment schedules, and disclosures.
  • Process and verify authorized payments and loan disbursements.
  • Manage repayments, customer support, complaints, and disputes.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our lending service.

3. Loan Decisions, Fees, and Disbursement

Loan applications are assessed according to the applicable eligibility criteria and lending terms. We will communicate the outcome and any relevant conditions to you.

Before you accept a loan or make a payment, we will disclose the applicable processing fee, interest and other charges where relevant, total repayment amount, repayment schedule, and applicable refund terms. A fee payment will be treated according to the loan agreement and the conditions communicated to you. Disbursement will be recorded only after the required approval, conditions, and payment or transfer verification have been completed.

We do not treat a payment request or an unverified transaction as proof that a loan has been disbursed. You should retain your official payment and disbursement confirmations.

4. Sharing of Information

Where necessary and lawful, we may share relevant information with payment service providers, identity-verification providers, authorized technology and hosting providers, professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement authorities, or credit reference bureaus where applicable and legally permitted.

We will limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary, apply appropriate safeguards, and provide any required notices or obtain any required authorization. We do not authorize service providers to use your information for unrelated purposes.

5. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. Access to customer applications and financial records should be restricted to authorized personnel. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this notice and for applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, dispute-resolution, and security requirements. Our retention procedures determine when information is securely deleted, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where permitted, restriction or objection to certain processing, and information about how your data is used. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to lawful limitations and any other applicable basis for processing.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.
